# Owner's grouped instructional stage layout — 3 November 2026

The owner requested this visual update after reviewing the scholarly `1.2.0-preview.1` application. That preview, local commit `f01c38aa8f14ea6f752db9a4c37ed94e3ffd0969`, is the implementation baseline. The public release remains 1.1.4.

## Requested arrangement

`[ [First] & [ {Inside} + {Outside} ] & [Last] ] = [Final Answer: ####]`

This is a **visual layout specification**, not literal mathematical notation. The ampersands represent visual connections between stages, not multiplication operations. The hierarchy must communicate **First → (Inside + Outside) → Last → Final Answer**.

First, Inside, Outside and Last remain separate instructional boxes. Inside and Outside are two distinct boxes visibly grouped in a larger middle section, with a plus sign between them. The completed answer is displayed in a clearly separated Final Answer box.

- First: bright, bold teal text on a lighter teal background.
- Inside and Outside: bright, bold muted-violet text on lighter violet backgrounds.
- Last: bright, bold warm-amber text on a lighter amber background.
- Approximately equal color prominence, with consistent font weights, borders, padding and contrast.
- Preserve the approved ivory `#FAF7F0`, scholarly typography, large clear mathematical numerals, restrained borders and spacing, with no distracting animation.
- On smaller screens, wrap or stack while retaining the grouping and relationships.
- Apply the colored boxes **only to Learn**. Practice remains monochrome.
- Preserve mathematical methods, arithmetic algorithms, lesson progression and all Practice behavior.
- Apply consistently to Web, iOS/iPadOS, macOS, Windows and Android, and provide representative screenshots for review **before publication or deployment**.

## Implementation interpretation

Arrows are used for the requested non-arithmetic visual connections; an explicit plus stays inside the middle enclosure, and an equals sign sits outside the stage enclosure before Final Answer. The grouping applies when the existing lesson supplies all four named stages. Other lessons retain their established numbered stages; no new multiplication stages or teaching methods are invented. Existing button actions, logical focus order, direct stage/final-answer selection, and Next/Back/carry progression remain unchanged.

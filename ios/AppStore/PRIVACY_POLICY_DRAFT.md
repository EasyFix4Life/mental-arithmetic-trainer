# Mental Arithmetic privacy policy — draft

Effective date: to be set when published.

Responsible developer: **owner to supply the legal person or organization and a contact method before publication**.

Mental Arithmetic provides arithmetic lessons and timed practice on your device. The current version works without an account or an internet connection.

## Information handled by the app

The app processes the arithmetic entries you type, whether each accepted answer is correct, and the elapsed time for each answer. It uses that information to advance the current round and show its score and maximum, minimum, and average answer times. Lesson selection and the current worked example are also held while the app is running.

These values remain in the application's memory for the current process. They are not transmitted to the developer, stored in an account, synchronized to a service, or maintained as a persistent practice history by this version. Ending the app's process clears this session state.

## Collection and sharing

The developer does not collect personal information through this app. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party data collection SDK, account login, or app-operated network service. It does not sell or share your arithmetic entries or timing results.

The app does not request access to contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, health information, or advertising identifiers. There is no app account to delete. Because this version sends no session data to the developer and does not retain it after the process ends, the developer has no stored practice record to retrieve or delete.

Apple may process information through operating-system, App Store, or diagnostic features under your Apple settings and Apple's own policies. This app does not add its own diagnostic upload service.

## Changes and contact

If a later version adds persistent storage, synchronization, analytics, or other data handling, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before that functionality is released.
